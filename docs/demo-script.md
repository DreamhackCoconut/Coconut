# 90-second Coconut demo

## 0–10 seconds — set the problem

“Small island artisans can reach global customers online, but shipping every seller’s orders independently is inefficient.”

Open the marketplace and point out **Island-made. Smarter shipped.**, independent makers, the shared shipment, and the next departure.

## 10–25 seconds — show the economics

Add **Handwoven Coastal Basket**. Open the cart and show:

- product price: $32.00
- estimated solo shipping: $28.20
- Coconut shared shipping: $19.12
- savings: $9.08

Say: “The product price stays with the artisan. Coconut changes the logistics price as compatible orders share fixed freight.”

## 25–40 seconds — show the Coconut moment

Point to **Shell Earrings** and the **+$0.00 shipping** explanation. Say: “Coconut evaluates physical packing and marginal shipping cost, not just customer similarity.” Add the earrings and show the quote remain in the same shipping bracket.

Expand **Why this?** to show parcel fit, shipping delta, readiness, and shared-batch reasons.

## 40–55 seconds — switch to Operations

Open Operations. Show artisan locations, the Friday West Coast batch, utilization, weather, and data-source badges.

## 55–70 seconds — optimize the pickup

Click **Optimize pickup route**. Show baseline vs the OR-Tools route, stop order, vehicle load, and the explanation that capacity and pickup windows were enforced.

## 70–80 seconds — show departure context

Point out departure choices and marine risk. Explain that weather-aware ranking helps the cooperative choose a reliable shared movement.

## 80–90 seconds — close on artisan value

Open the Artisan dashboard. Show the production queue, market opportunities, and price guidance.

Close: “Coconut makes dozens of tiny island exporters behave like one coordinated logistics network.”
