# Why Coconut is different

1. **The island constraint is fundamental.** Geography, small volumes, fixed freight, departures, and marine conditions shape the product.
2. **Recommendations understand logistics.** Relevance is balanced with physical parcel fit and marginal shipping cost.
3. **Dynamic pricing uses shared-cost economics.** Seller prices stay independent while logistics prices respond to a fuller compatible batch.
4. **Packing affects commerce decisions.** Cartonization is an input to discovery, not a hidden afterthought.
5. **OR-Tools performs real constrained routing.** Capacity, volume, windows, service time, and cutoff are enforced.
6. **Marine weather affects departure decisions.** Reliability is shown beside cost and speed.
7. **Seller fairness matters.** One merchant should not dominate recommendations simply because it has more history.
8. **The system is explainable.** Recommendation, route, departure, market, and price guidance decisions expose reasons.
9. **The demo is visual.** The cart, map, utilization, and artisan queue make the network effect easy to present.
10. **No generic chatbot is required.** Coconut’s intelligence is packing, ranking, optimization, and decision support grounded in real constraints.
